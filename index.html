I’m a curious builder drawn to both the structure of technology and the feeling of art.

I enjoy exploring how digital experiences can be practical without feeling ordinary. Some days that means thinking through a product flow; other days it means getting lost in typography, visual composition or a new creative idea.

This little atelier is a record of that ongoing process — the things I build, the things I learn and the ideas still taking shape.
